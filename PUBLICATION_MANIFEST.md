# Publication manifest

This manifest records what was intentionally included or excluded when preparing the public repository.

## Included

- Generic interdisciplinary meeting and discussion workflow
- Reusable presentation, meeting-note, and discussion templates
- Generic GitHub reading and writing rules for AI assistants
- Public-repository connection test instructions
- One fully synthetic interpretation-gap example

## Adapted before inclusion

- The workflow was rewritten to avoid claiming that the complete online-discussion cycle succeeded.
- Repository connection instructions were rewritten without private repository names, accounts, infrastructure, or real issues.
- Scientific examples were replaced with synthetic measurement-boundary examples.

## Excluded

- All meeting notes and attendance records
- All photographs and binary project assets
- Reports, goal-attainment evaluations, and internal synthesis logs
- Financial or administrative evidence
- Collaborator correspondence and account lists
- Unpublished manuscripts, figures, and project-specific scientific material
- Private onboarding instructions involving shared accounts, servers, VPNs, SSH, tokens, or institutional infrastructure

## Publication rule

Future content should be added directly as public-safe material. Do not synchronize this repository automatically from the private archive.
